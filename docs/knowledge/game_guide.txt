# Navigation Guide
- Pallet Town: Go NORTH (UP) through the grass to reach Route 1.
- Route 1: Go NORTH (UP) to reach Viridian City.
- Viridian City: Go NORTH (UP) to reach Route 2 and Viridian Forest.
- Terrain: You can walk on GRASS. It may trigger battles, which are good for leveling up.
- Terrain: You cannot walk on TREES, WATER (without Surf), or LEDGES (from the wrong side).
- Ledges: You can jump over ledges by walking DOWN, but you cannot walk UP them.

# Key Items Locations
- HM01 (Cut): SS Anne Captain's room in Vermilion City.
- HM02 (Fly): Route 16, cut the tree and go through the gate, speak to the girl in the house.
- HM03 (Surf): Safari Zone, Area 4, inside the Secret House.
- HM04 (Strength): Fuchsia City, give the Gold Teeth to the Warden.
- HM05 (Flash): Route 2, through Diglett's Cave, speak to Oak's Aide (requires 10 Pokemon).
- Bicycle: Cerulean City Bike Shop (requires Bike Voucher).
- Bike Voucher: Vermilion City Fan Club, listen to the Chairman.
- Gold Teeth: Safari Zone, Area 4.
- Silph Scope: Celadon City, Rocket Hideout (Game Corner basement), defeat Giovanni.
- Poke Flute: Lavender Town, Pokemon Tower, defeat Team Rocket at the top (requires Silph Scope).
- Old Rod: Vermilion City, Fishing Guru.
- Good Rod: Fuchsia City, Fishing Guru's brother.
- Super Rod: Route 12, Fishing Guru's brother.

# Gym Leaders
- Brock (Pewter): Rock type. Weak directly to Water, Grass.
- Misty (Cerulean): Water type. Weak to Electric, Grass.
- Lt. Surge (Vermilion): Electric type. Weak to Ground.
- Erika (Celadon): Grass type. Weak to Fire, Ice, Flying.
- Koga (Fuchsia): Poison type. Weak to Psychic, Ground.
- Sabrina (Saffron): Psychic type. Weak to Bug.
- Blaine (Cinnabar): Fire type. Weak to Water, Ground.
- Giovanni (Viridian): Ground type. Weak to Water, Grass, Ice.
